THE STORY BEHIND THE GUIDE

Why I Built This Guide

The guide I wish existed when I started.

I wanted to use an iPhone, FreeMoCap, Blender, and Mixamo to create motion-captured animations. I expected the hardest part to be animation. It wasn't. The hardest part was getting everything working together.

  1. 01

    THE IDEA

    I wanted to make motion-captured animations

    I wanted to record motion on an iPhone, process it with FreeMoCap, bring it into Blender, and retarget it onto a Mixamo character. I thought the most difficult part would be the animation itself.

    It wasn't. The hardest part was getting each tool, setting, and file to work with the next one.

  2. 02

    THE SETUP

    The small setup problems kept adding up

    I ran into FFmpeg not being recognized, PATH issues, unsupported video formats, import failures, and calibration steps I didn't yet understand. I also had to work out how each stage connected: what started in the phone video, what FreeMoCap produced, and how that motion could reach a Blender character.

  3. 03

    THE SEARCH

    The information existed, but it was scattered

    I spent hours moving between YouTube videos, GitHub discussions, documentation pages, forum posts, Reddit threads, and troubleshooting guides. One source might explain FFmpeg, another FreeMoCap, another Mixamo, and a video might cover Blender retargeting. When something went wrong, I could end up with five tabs open just to solve one problem.

“I wish there was one guide that showed the entire workflow from start to finish.”

So I built it.

This website is the guide I wished existed when I started: one place to follow the whole process, understand how the steps fit together, and find a next thing to try when something goes wrong.

One connected workflow

The point is not to make every tool feel simple on its own. It is to show how the pieces connect, from the first recording to the final animation.

Mistakes I Made So You Don't Have To

These are some of the snags I wish I had known to check sooner:

  • FFmpeg not on PATH
  • Unsupported video formats
  • Using extremely low-resolution video
  • Import failures
  • Calibration confusion

A companion to the official documentation

This site is not meant to replace the excellent FreeMoCap, Blender, Mixamo, or FFmpeg documentation. Those resources remain the official source for current details.

Instead, this guide brings the whole workflow together in one place, so a beginner can see where a step fits and where to go next.

If This Guide Saves You Time

That is why I put the pieces together. Happy animating.
